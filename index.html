Welcome to my Room Portfolio

Hi, I'm Yahir Bernal! This website is a 3D room that holds my portfolio. Instead of scrolling through pages, you can explore the room and click on objects to learn about my music, artwork, computer projects, and engineering projects. Room inspired by Andrew Hoans room portfolio. All artwork, music, and 3D models are modeled by me. Enjoy exploring!

How it works

  • Look around by clicking and dragging anywhere in the room.
  • Zoom in and out with your scroll wheel or trackpad.
  • Hover over objects. The ones that pop up are interactive.
  • Click an interactive object to zoom in and open its section.
  • Close a section with the X button to return to the full room view.
  • In the artwork section, click any piece to view it full size.

Heads up

This website is still a work in progress! Some sections are unfinished, and content, visuals, and features will keep changing as I continue to build it out.
